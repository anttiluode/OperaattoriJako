THE DIAGNOSTIC
Full = transport + feedback.
Transport differentiates P and X while replaying the exact base current waveform. Feedback is the extra tangent created by dJ/dV through the local implicit loop. At 1× in this archived cell-1125 example, transport-only length is 0 positive / 31 negative, while the full nonlinear tangent is 7 positive / 24 negative: feedback creates the seven proximal sign reversals.
from jako import split_operator_tangent

r = split_operator_tangent(
    P, X, dP, dX,
    site_nodes,
    output_node,
    base_current,
    base_dJdV,
)

transport = r.transport
feedback  = r.feedback
full      = r.full
